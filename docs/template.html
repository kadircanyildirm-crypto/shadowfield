Under the hood

Three steps

01 · Orbit

Physics for every frame

From the public TLE: sunlight now and over the last 200 minutes, minutes since sunrise or sunset, orbital phase, position and velocity, Earth's magnetic field.

02 · Bytes

Every reading tested

Each offset, width, byte order, sign and float is correlated with that physics, week by week, with significance counted in passes. Structure tests settle the layout: carries, sign extension, the wrap point.

03 · Claude

Names from evidence

Claude reads each field's response shape, time constant and range, blind to the satellite. Three samples, majority answer: name, unit, scale, and a health note.

The output

A decoder file, written for you

Everything found above, as a Kaitai Struct draft: the format SatNOGS decoders are written in. It compiles with the official compiler and reads real frames exactly as the engine does.
