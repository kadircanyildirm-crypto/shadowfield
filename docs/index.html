Shadowfield

Hundreds of small satellites send telemetry nobody can read: no public format, just bytes. Shadowfield works out what the bytes mean from the orbit alone. A battery charges in sunlight, a panel cools in Earth's shadow, a clock keeps time, a GNSS fix follows the orbit. Then Claude names each field like a spacecraft engineer. The satellite's decoder and its name are never used.
