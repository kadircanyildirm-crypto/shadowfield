How it works

Three steps, no decoder

01

Physics from the orbit

For every frame, the public TLE gives sunlight, the last 200 minutes of sunlight history, minutes since sunrise or sunset, orbital phase, position and velocity, and the IGRF magnetic field.

02

Every reading, tested

Each offset, width, byte order, sign and float is correlated with that physics, within weeks, with significance counted in passes. Structural tests then settle the byte layout (carries, sign extension).

03

Claude reads the evidence

Blind to the satellite's name, Claude reads each field's response shape, time constant and range, and proposes name, unit and scale. It is asked three times and the majority answer is kept.
