A 89-byte beacon frame with several multi-byte counters and fields that look like they come from the same reboot-driven process. Every candidate field jumps at the same ~10 reset events, so they seem to be uptime or counter values that restart at reboot, not sensors tracking sunlight. Health. The counters show 10 resets in 150 days, with the last about 60 days before the end. This suggests repeated reboots earlier in the window and a quieter period since, though a wrap by design cannot be excluded. There is no usable battery or thermal evidence in these fields.
