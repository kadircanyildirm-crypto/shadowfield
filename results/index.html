Shadowfield: blind decoding scoreboard

Telemetry fields recovered from raw SatNOGS frames using only orbital physics (from the TLE) and Claude, without the satellite's decoder or name. The published satnogs-decoders definitions are used afterwards, only to grade. Recall: share of the decoder's physical fields (temperature, power, clock, counter, navigation, magnetometer) that were found. Exact: the found reading reproduces the decoder's raw values bit for bit (offset, width, byte order, sign). Precision: share of reported fields that are real decoder fields. Kind: the engine's physics class matches the decoder's category. Claude: Claude's blind category matches (an "unknown" answer counts as wrong); answered: share not marked unknown; when answered: accuracy on those; battery / solar / bus: the finer power class, where the decoder's field name says which. Each Claude answer is the majority of three independent samples.
