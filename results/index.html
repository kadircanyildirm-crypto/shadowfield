Shadowfield: blind decoding scoreboard

Telemetry fields recovered from raw SatNOGS frames using only orbital physics (from the TLE) and Claude, without the satellite's decoder or name. The published satnogs-decoders definitions are used afterwards, only to grade. Recall: share of the decoder's physical fields (temperatures, power, clocks, counters, magnetometers) that were found. Exact: found at exactly the decoder's byte offset and width. Precision: share of reported fields that are real decoder fields. Kind: engine's physics class matches the decoder's category. Claude: Claude's blind category for the field matches the decoder's.
