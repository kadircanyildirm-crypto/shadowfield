Frame type DP0UWH:len58

1105 frames, 411 passes, 58-byte payload

58-byte housekeeping beacon with a 4-byte uptime-like counter, a millisecond clock, a 3-byte counter, a few small bytes, then power/thermal blocks. Contains what looks like two battery or solar-channel voltage/current pairs, a 3-voltage/current-like group, and four panel temperatures at offsets 49-53.

Health. Counters at offsets 2 and 27 reset 11 times in 44.8 days, with the latest 3.7 days before the end, which suggests repeated reboots. The clock at offset 15 reset once, 3.6 days before the end, and is probably the same event. The battery voltage daily lows fall by about 70-80 mV per 30 days, which may point to ageing or a seasonal effect, so I am not sure. Panel temperatures swing about 50 degC between sun and shadow, which is normal.
