Every field below was found without a decoder: candidate byte readings were tested against sunlight, thermal lag, orbital phase, wall-clock time and the IGRF magnetic field computed from the TLE. Names, units and scales are Claude's reading of that evidence, with the satellite name withheld.

Frame type GBSAT0:len68

897 frames, 94 passes, 68-byte payload

A 68-byte beacon (GBSAT0 header) with two free-running 1-byte sequence counters, a 3-byte uptime clock, and a block of power and thermal fields near the end of the frame. Solar input and battery-related fields respond to eclipse, with two small quantities that are higher in shadow.

Health. No clock resets were seen (uptime is continuous over 25 days), so there is no sign of reboots; the 1-byte counters wrap by design. Battery voltage daily low drifts slightly downward, but 25 days include season effects, so the evidence for ageing is weak. Battery current shows discharge in shadow and charge in sun, which is normal.
